Rulebook

Server Rules

The complete rulebook for the Beacon Bot community. Read carefully — ignorance is not a defence.

General
01

Be Respectful

Treat every member and staff member with respect. Hate speech, discrimination, harassment, or toxic behavior will not be tolerated.

02

No Evasion

Attempting to bypass bans, mutes, or other moderation actions with alt accounts or exploits will result in further action.

Moderation
03

Fair Enforcement

Staff must act consistently and document significant moderation decisions. Members deserve clear explanations when action is taken.

04

No Spam or Raids

Do not flood channels, mass-mention members, or coordinate disruptive activity against the server.

05

Appeals Welcome

If you believe a moderation decision was unfair, submit an appeal with honest context rather than arguing in public channels.
