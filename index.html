KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Server rules

These guidelines keep your Discord community safe and welcoming for everyone.

ON THIS PAGE01. General conduct02. Moderation standards03. Enforcement & appeals04. Discord & communication
01

General conduct

Be respectful. Harassment, hate speech, discrimination and targeted toxicity are never welcome in Beacon Bot communities.

  • Keep discussions civil, even when you disagree.
  • Do not attempt to bypass moderation actions or evade bans.
  • Follow directions from staff while a situation is being reviewed.
02

Moderation standards

Staff actions should be fair, consistent, and documented. Every member deserves a clear explanation when action is taken.

  • Warn before escalating when the situation allows.
  • Do not abuse staff permissions for personal disputes.
  • Log significant actions for accountability.
03

Enforcement & appeals

Bans, mutes, and warnings exist to protect the community. Members may appeal decisions they believe were unfair.

04

Discord & communication

Use appropriate channels, respect staff decisions, and keep support requests in tickets rather than public arguments.
